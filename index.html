About

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University. My current work focuses on controlled empirical studies of model behavior, with a particular interest in AI agents and reasoning systems.

A recurring question in my research is why a model can notice, describe, or even explicitly acknowledge relevant information without that information reliably changing what it does next. I am interested in studying these gaps behaviorally and using them to inform better evaluation, training, and cognitive architectures for AI.

Research

Research interests

AI Agents & Model Behavior

Behavioral regularities in tool-using and coding agents, including action allocation, adaptation, persistence, and decision-making under changing evidence.

Reasoning & Revision

When additional computation, reflection, or feedback changes an operative hypothesis — and when it only produces local repair without genuine revision.

Evaluation & Experimental Design

Controlled experiments and evaluation environments that separate surface success from the mechanisms and behavioral changes that produce it.

Cognitive Architectures for AI

How capabilities needed for robust execution — such as evaluation, memory, adaptation, and control — can become native parts of learned systems rather than only external scaffolding.

I am especially interested in questions at the boundary between machine learning, cognitive science, and interactive intelligent systems.
